QUESTIONS, BEFORE THE SIGNAL
What does support fund?

Hosting, writing, design, access-agent experiments, and the time it takes to keep books free or discounted for more readers.

Can I contribute a book?

Yes. Approved FED-HUB users can upload Markdown directly from the contributor lane without a second approval step.

Is a contribution required to read?

No. The access agent is designed to keep every title free or discounted. Support is optional and helps us keep that promise.
